Our Story

Made with love,
worn with confidence.

BluesGloss started as a bedroom experiment and became a movement. We believe your lips are the punctuation mark of every look — so we make sure that mark is always perfect.

Since 2022

A glossy idea that grew into something real.

It started in 2022, when our founder couldn't find a lip gloss that did everything she wanted — long-lasting wear, actually nourishing ingredients, and shades that worked for all skin tones. So she made her own. Right here in Nigeria 🇳🇬.

What began as weekend batches at home became BluesGloss — a full collection of lip products that refuse to compromise. "Blues" isn't just a colour here. It's a feeling. That deep, confident, show-stopping feeling you get when your lips look exactly right.

Today, BluesGloss delivers nationwide across Nigeria — Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano, and everywhere in between. We've built a community of over 12,000 loyal customers who swear by the formula and share their looks on TikTok and Instagram every day.

Every product is formulated with clean, safe ingredients that work with your skin, not against it. We're cruelty-free, and always working to make our packaging more eco-friendly. Because great beauty shouldn't come at a cost — not to you, and not to the planet.

What We Stand For

Three things we'll never compromise on

Clean Beauty First

Every formula is free from parabens, sulphates, phthalates, and synthetic fragrances. What goes on your lips matters — we take that seriously.

Cruelty-Free Always

Not just a label for us. We work only with suppliers who share our values. PETA-certified and proud of it. Kindness is the only beauty standard we care about.

Made for Everyone

Our shades are developed with all skin tones in mind. If a gloss doesn't look stunning on every complexion, it doesn't make it into the collection. Simple.

FAQ

Got questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know before you glow. Can't find what you're looking for? Just drop us a message.

All of our lip glosses and balms are formulated with clean, skin-loving ingredients including vitamin E, jojoba oil, and natural waxes. We never use parabens, phthalates, sulphates, or synthetic fragrances. Full ingredient lists are listed on each product page, because we believe you deserve to know exactly what you're putting on your skin.
100% cruelty-free. We are PETA certified and never test on animals at any stage of production. Most of our range is also vegan — check individual product pages for confirmation. Our Honey Dew Balm contains beeswax and is therefore not vegan, but all other balms and glosses are fully plant-based.
Our glosses are formulated for 4–6 hours of comfortable wear under normal conditions. Eating and drinking will require a touch-up, as with any gloss. For the longest wear, apply to clean, exfoliated lips and blot once after initial application before adding a second layer. The lip balms are designed for all-day hydration — not just a surface glow.

Start with hydrated, exfoliated lips — our lip balms are great for prep! Apply gloss starting from the centre of your upper lip, following the cupid's bow, then fill in. For a plumper effect, apply a touch more in the centre of your bottom lip. Avoid pressing your lips together to set — let the gloss settle naturally for the glossiest finish. Less is more on the first application.

Store your BluesGloss products in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat. Avoid leaving them in a hot car — heat can change the texture of glosses and melt balms. Shelf life is 12 months from opening (indicated by the open jar symbol on the packaging). If a product smells or looks different, don't use it — contact us and we'll help.
Our formulas are dermatologically tested and free from common irritants. However, if you have known allergies or very sensitive skin, we always recommend doing a patch test on the inside of your wrist before use. If you have any specific concerns about an ingredient, don't hesitate to get in touch — our team can advise based on your individual needs.
